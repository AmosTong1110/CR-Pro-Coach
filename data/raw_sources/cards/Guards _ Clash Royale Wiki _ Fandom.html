“Three ruthless bone brothers with shields. Knock off their shields and all that's left are three ruthless bone brothers.”

The Guards is an Epic card that is unlocked from the P.E.K.K.A's Playhouse (Arena 6). It spawns three fast single-target, ground-targeting, melee, ground troops with low hitpoints and low damage with shields in addition to their normal hitpoints. Their deployment is in a triangle formation. They appear to be Skeletons, but are somewhat taller, wear black helmets with team-colored feathers, and carry spears and wooden shields with a crown emblem on them. A Guards card costs 3 Elixir to deploy.

Strategy[]

For Battle Decks with this card, click here.

  • The Guards function similar to Skeletons with triple the cost, but are more reliable defensively thanks to their shields improving their resistance against enemy spells or any other powerful attack, as any excess damage is negated.
    • If Lightning is used on Guards, only the shields will be destroyed. This is because the shield and the unit are both considered the same entity.
  • Guards are best used as a defensive card, then used in a counterpush with something that can tank for them, such as a Miner, Ice Golem, or a Hog Rider.
  • Their defensive potential marks them as a low-risk, high-reward option. They can eliminate ground troops like groups of Skeletons and can distract air troops like Minions, making them a good pick against an incoming enemy push for gaining positive Elixir trades. They don't have much damage or pushing power for 3 Elixir, so they should be rarely used on offense; they could even fail to take out tanky troops like the Royal Giant quickly.
  • The Guards can be used effectively to counter a lone Sparky. With their shield, they can withstand a blow from the Sparky and destroy her before she shoots a second time. For better results, surround her by spawning them directly on her so only one Guard gets hit with the blast. They will quickly destroy Sparky with the help of a Crown Tower, leaving the player two Guards with shields and one without.
  • The vast majority of single-target troops or those with slow attacks can be easily countered by Guards, including Prince, P.E.K.K.A, Giant Skeleton, Boss Bandit, and Mini P.E.K.K.A. They are especially effective against ones that cannot destroy their shields in one hit, such as Knight, Musketeer, Bandit, Battle Healer, Archer Queen, Electro Wizard, Golden Knight, and Fisherman.
  • Guards are able to counter Mega Knight if placed dirrectly on top of him circling his so he only hit one at a time. This must be done before his jump animation. Proper placement is mandatory and will not fully countered if not placed right.
  • Despite having a shield, certain spells are still effective counters to Guards.
    • The Log can make short work of the Guards since it knocks off the Guards' shields, making them weak enough for the Crown Tower to finish them off. An Evolved Zap can be used similarly.
    • Poison can be used to completely take them out, due to the 8 ticks of Poison being more than capable of taking their shield out first and finishing them off second. It takes 3 seconds for it to destroy the shields, while an additional one is required for the Guards themselves.
    • If the Guards are alone, they can be destroyed entirely with the Void for a neutral Elixir trade. The shields will be taken down with 2 of the strikes, while the last one will finish off the Guards. The player can deploy any card that spawns 2 or more troops to save the Guards from the spell, even preventing their shield from being removed.
    • While they cannot fully destroy them, Arrows and Royal Delivery have the same Elixir cost and enough damage to destroy the Guards' shields. The latter will also spawn a Royal Recruit to assist with further defense against them.
    • The Barbarian Barrel can be used similarly to the Royal Delivery and for 1 less Elixir. However, it is unable to destroy the shields by itself and will require the assistance of the Barbarian to do so.
  • The Guards can be countered with area damage troops or buildings. Cards like Bomber, Royal Ghost, Ice Wizard, Firecracker, and Princess are most effective, while the Wizard, Valkyrie, Executioner, Dark Prince, Witch, Magic Archer, Bowler, Mega Knight, Goblin Demolisher, Skeleton King, or Bomb Tower are also good, though expensive, alternative options.
    • Although not dealing area damage, the Electro Dragon will be able to hit all three of the Guards with one attack. It takes it 3 attacks to fully destroy the Guards. Although it is a negative Elixir trade, it will have full hitpoints for a counterattack.
    • The Hunter's attack can bypass the shields allowing him to deal with the Guards easily. His first attack will be able to fully destroy 2 Guards, and the next one will finish off the last Guard. After that, he will be ready for a counterattack.
  • Guards cannot target air units, so cheap air troops like Bats or Minions can quickly defeat them. The Baby Dragon and Skeleton Dragons are especially effective due to being able to hit multiple Guards at once.
  • The Fire Spirit, Ice Spirit, Heal Spirit, and Electro Spirit do not deal enough damage to take out the Guards' shields but they do deal enough damage to finish off the Guards themselves. Knowing this, you can combine either of them with The Log to fully counter the Guards for a neutral Elixir trade, however that requires precise timing.
    • Ice Spirit can be used to counter them, freezing them and allowing your Crown Tower to finish them off.
  • Guards are great for defending against the Graveyard, since they are more durable than regular Skeletons and have a longer range. For similar reasons, they are also effective at countering the Miner, capable of tanking multiple hits from him.
  • The Guards can finish off a weakened Golem reliably. Their shields will allow them survive the Golem's death damage, and they will quickly destroy the Golemites. However, they will likely be defeated rather quickly if the Golem is supported by other cards, and the Guards will be destroyed entirely after dealing with the Golemites.
  • The Guards are a great support card for an X-Bow or a Mortar. Their shields allow them to distract enemy troops long enough, buying for time for the siege building to deal damage to its target.
  • When the Three Musketeers are coming to a Crown Tower without support, once the player's Crown Tower targets the Three Musketeers, they can do a quick drop of Guards and then Ice Spirit. The first Guard can negate three simultaneous hits and then the Ice Spirit will freeze them for 1.2 seconds which is enough time for the 2 remaining Guards and Crown Tower to finish off the Musketeers.
  • An very powerful defensive combination is the Guards paired with the Ice Wizard. The Ice Wizard slows down enemy troops while chipping them down, while the Guards distract and damage the troops, benefitting immensely from the Ice Wizard’s slow improving their survivability. This combo can be further enhanced by high-DPS ranged units like the Cannon and Musketeer, who will appreciate the amount of free time generated from the combo.
  • Guards are actually able to slightly outrange some troops because of their spears, similar to the Prince. Because of this, they can poke at a distracted Valkyrie without taking any damage, setting you up for a counterpush.
  • The Guards' shields allows them to be a great meatshield against an Inferno Tower, Inferno Dragon, or a Mighty Miner. These three cards will reset their charge after they destroy a shield, being distracted for longer.
  • Depending on the player's needs in a deck, the Guards can be replaced with other similar cards.
    • The Guards are a good alternative to Barbarians if the player needs to keep their deck cheap as they cost 2 less Elixir. The trade-off is that the Guards have all-around lower statistics except for speed.
    • For a lower Elixir cost, Skeletons and Goblins can work as a replacement for Guards, with the latter also being better than Guards offensively due to their higher movement speed. However, the lack of shields on both units makes them worse defensively, especially against spells.
    • Skeleton Army and Goblin Gang offer significantly higher damage compared to Guards for the same Elixir cost. However, much like the previous cards, they are easier to counter with spells, due to their lack of shields.
    • If the player can afford to defend without a counterpush afterwards, they can use the Tombstone as a replacemet for the Guards for the same Elixir cost. While weak to spells, it can also distract building-targeting troops like the Giant, Battle Ram, Ram Rider, Wall Breakers, Elixir Golem, and Hog Rider.

History[]

2016

  • The Guards card was added to the game on 3/5/2016, in the May 2016 Update.
  • On 18/5/2016, a Balance Update, increased the Guards' hitpoints and damage by 8%.
  • On 21/6/2016, a Balance Update, removed the Guards' pushback when their shields break.

2017

  • On 9/10/2017, the Epic Quests Update, fixed a bug where the Inferno Tower's and Inferno Dragon's damage did not reset after breaking the Guards' shield. This change was reverted on 11/10/2017, in a maintenance break.
  • On 12/12/2017, the Electrifying Update, increased the Guards’ hitpoints and damage by 5% and decreased their attack time interval to 1.1 seconds (from 1.2 seconds). It also made it so that the Inferno Tower's and Inferno Dragon's would now reset after breaking the Guards' shield.

2018

  • On 5/9/2018, the September 2018 Update, changed it so that Epic cards start at Level 6 and end at Level 13 (from 1 to 8).

2020

  • On 2/6/2020, a Balance Update, gave the Guards a new 0.1 seconds deploy stagger from each other.
  • On 9/12/2020, a Balance Update, decreased the Guards' attack time interval to 1 second (from 1.1 seconds) and decreased their hitpoints by 26%.

2021

  • On 7/6/2021, the Summer 2021 Update, moved the Arena to unlock the Guards from Royal Arena to Spooky Town.
  • On 2/9/2021, a maintenance break, added a card render to the Guards' card info screen.
  • On 6/9/2021, the 2021 Quarter 3 Update, increased the Guards' damage by 12%.
  • On 27/10/2021, the Champions Update, increased the Level cap of all cards to 14 (from 13).

2022

  • On 26/10/2022, the 2022 Quarter 3 Update, moved the Arena to unlock the Guards from Spooky Town to P.E.K.K.A's Playhouse.

2023

  • On 19/6/2023, the Card Evolution Update, increased the Level cap of all cards to 15 (from 14).

2024

  • On 3/1/2024, a Balance Update, increased the Guards' shield hitpoints by 7%.

2025

  • On 31/3/2025, the 2025 Quarter 1 Update, increased the Guards' damage by 0.82%. It also made it so that Guards' spawn formation would be correctly mirrored depending on which side of the Arena they were placed.
  • On 5/5/2025, the May 2025 Update, decreased the Guards' damage by 4%.
  • On 4/8/2025, the August 2025 Update made it so that the Evolved Witch would no longer be healed by defeated Guards.
  • On 6/10/2025, a Balance Update, decreased the Guards' First Hit Speed to 0.5 second (from 0.4 seconds)

Trivia[]

  • Shields were first developed during the Bronze Age, as a protection device, designed to block attacks by hand weapons, such as swords, axes and maces, or ranged weapons like sling-stones and arrows. Shields have varied greatly in construction over time and place. Sometimes shields were made of metal, but wood or animal hide construction was much more common; wicker and even turtle shells have been used. Shields were retired in warfare, but are still used to this day for police enforcement.
    • The Guards are 1 of 3 troops to have a shield, along with the Royal Recruits and Dark Prince.
      • The Guards are the only troop to have more shield hitpoints than their own hitpoints.
      • The Guards are 1 of 2 troops with more damage than hitpoints, the other being Wall Breakers.
    • In the Dutch and Russian language settings of the game, the Guards' name translates to "Guardians". In the Turkish, Japanese, and Malaysian language settings of the game, the Guards' name translates to "Protectors", "Shield Warriors", and "Controllers" respectively.
  • While not available as a troop in Clash of Clans, the Guards are similar to the Skeletons spawned from a Skeleton Spell.
  • The Guards are 1 of 15 cards associated with Skeletons, with the others being Bomber, Skeleton Dragons, Skeleton Barrel, Tombstone, Bomb Tower, Skeleton Army, Witch, Balloon, Giant Skeleton, Wall Breakers, Super Witch, Graveyard, Skeleton King, and of course, Skeletons.
    • In the Chinese language settings of the game, the Guards' name translates to "Skeleton Guards".
  • The Guards are tied with Skeletons, Bats, and Suspicious Bush for the lowest hitpoints of all troops in the game, with 118 hitpoints at Level 15. However, unlike the other two, they also come with a shield.
  • The Guards have been featured in multiple special game modes outside of the card. More specifically:
    • The Guards are 1 of 23 troops that have been featured in the Troop Rush game mode, the others being Skeletons, Skeleton Dragons, Fire Spirit, Bats, Elite Barbarians, Firecracker, Rascal Girls, Royal Hogs, Heal Spirit, Elixir Golem, Prince, Balloon, Golem, Wall Breakers, Ram Rider, Sparky, Phoenix, Royal Ghost, Bandit, Lumberjack, Santa Hog Rider, and the Monk.
      • Along with Skeleton Dragons, the Guards in the Troop Rush game mode would spawn in random positions instead of behind the Crown Towers.
    • The Guards are 1 of 14 troops that has been featured with a Rage buff, the others being Electro Spirit, Giant, Zappies, Giant Skeleton, Goblin Giant, Electro Dragon, Wall Breakers, Electro Giant, Mega Knight, Ram Rider, Electro Wizard, Sparky, and Royal Ghost.
  • They appear to have an underbite, similar to the Minions, Giant Skeleton, Lava Hound, and Super Lava Hound.
  • The Guards, along with the Minions, Bomber, Skeletons, Skeleton Dragons, Ice Spirit, Mini P.E.K.K.A, Mega Minion, Ice Golem, Dart Goblin, Elixir Golem, Goblin Brawler, Baby Dragon, P.E.K.K.A, Giant Skeleton, Golem, Wall Breakers, Bowler, Executioner, Mega Knight, Inferno Dragon, Royal Ghost, and Lava Hound, are featured as a monster in Squad Busters.
    • An ice variant and a lava variant for the Guard are also featured as a monster in Squad Buster.
    • According to their description in Squad Busters, the Guards are the King's bodyguards
  • The Card Mastery task "Pain's An Old Friend" is a reference to a quote mentioned by Dr. Strange.
  • The Guards are the subject of an Emote.
    • The Guard Crown Emote is a reference to the Clash of Clans Lost & Crowned commercial.
  • The Guards were featured in the winning deck of the Clash Royale League 2022 Tournament, by a player called Mohamed Light.
  • The initial balance change for Guards on the May 2025 Update was a 6% damage decrease, but it was lowered to 4% last minute.
