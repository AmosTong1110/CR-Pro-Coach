“With every pinecone a target and every branch a new vantage point, Hero Magic Archer was favored to be Forest Gang's first Hero. Being Boss Bandit's right hand comes with a few perks!”

The Heroic Magic Archer is a Common card that is unlocked with 200 . It spawns an area damage, air-targeting, long-ranged, ground troop with moderate hitpoints and moderate damage. The Magic Archer launches an attack that travels up to its maximum range, while piercing through enemies. He takes the appearance of a man with glowing yellow eyes, blonde hair with a team-colored stripe on the front side, bushy eyebrows and sideburns, wears a dark green tunic and pants, a beige cape with a gold lining and a Hero Shard on the center, a brown belt with a triangular copper rune and fingerless gloves with gold cuffs and a beige guard with a gold lining on the right, and dark brown boots with a gold handle on each, and wields a glowing yellow bow with beige stabilizers with gold trimmings. He also appears to be carrying a keychain on his waist that appears to be the Boss Bandit. A Heroic Magic Archer card costs 3 to deploy.

Ability: Triple Threat[]

“Summons a decoy while darting back and fires a triple shot on his next attack.”
File:Ability-Triple Threat.png

The Hero Magic Archer has a special ability accessible from whatever side of the screen was put in, just above the player's card slots in battle. When activated, the Magic Archer will teleport back, while leaving a decoy in his place. The next attack now shots 3 arrows that travel longer, but with less damage. The Triple Threat ability costs 2 Elixir to activate.

Strategy[]

  • The ability can be used to avoid damage from spells like Fireball or units like the Mini P.E.K.K.A. or Prince, thanks to the teleport and decoy.
  • The arrows can deal good chip damage to Towers.

History[]

2026

  • On 2/3/2026, with the March 2026 update, Hero Magic Archer Was added to the game along with Hero Barbarian Barrel.
  • On 7/4/2026, a Balance Update, increased the Hero Magic Archer's ability cost to 2 elixir (from 1 elixir).
  • On 6/7/2026, a Balance Update, decreased the Hero Magic Archer's ability range to 13.5 tiles (from 15.4 tiles), decreased his teleportation distance to 3.5 tiles (from 5 tiles), and decreased the decoy hitpoints by 57%.
  • On 4/8/2026, a Balance Update, decreased the Hero Magic Archer's damage by 6%. This also made the ability single use (from 25sec cooldown).

Trivia[]

  • The word "Archer" comes from the Latin "arcus", meaning bow. The practice of archery was first developed in Africa by the later Middle Stone Age (approx. 70,000 years ago). It is documented as part of warfare and hunting from the classical period until the late medieval period when it was made obsolete by the increased use of firearms.
    • The bow that Magic Archer uses in battle is a magical recurve bow.
    • In the Persian language setting of the game, the Magic Archer's name translates to "Magic Shooter".
  • Before release the Hero Magic Archer's Decoy hitpoints got reduced by 9%, while increasing the cooldown of Triple Threat to 25 seconds (from 20 seconds).
